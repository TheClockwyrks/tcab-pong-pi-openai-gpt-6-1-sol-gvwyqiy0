# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. No straight answers. Carom turns the familiar paddle duel
into a contest of angles, timing, and beautifully crooked shots. Take on the
machine in Solo, or share a keyboard for a local Versus grudge match.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Then the rally starts to
accelerate: every paddle hit adds speed, and a strike made while moving gives
the ball spin. Brush upward or drive downward at contact and watch the flight
bend after it has left your paddle.

Two bright obstacles sway and rotate between you and the far goal. Their tilted
faces can rescue a lost ball or turn a safe return into a wicked bank shot. Read
their motion, play off the walls, and make your opponent defend a path that keeps
changing.

A point buys a breath, not a victory. First to eleven takes the match, but you
must lead by two. At deuce, each patient return and last-second change of angle
can decide everything. Find your angle. Put a little spin on it.
